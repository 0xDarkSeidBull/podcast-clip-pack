## 3. Try to double-spend and you get caught

- **File:** [`clips/03-accountable-consensus.mp4`](clips/03-accountable-consensus.mp4) (subtitles also as [`subtitles/03-accountable-consensus.srt`](subtitles/03-accountable-consensus.srt))

- **Timestamps:** 11:48.7-11:56.2, 12:16.4-12:41.5, 12:42.2-12:57.6, 13:02.2-13:10.5
- **Speaker:** Vincent Gramoli, Founder, Redbelly Network
- **Topic:** Redbelly technology (accountable consensus)
- **Suggested post caption:** You can try to double-spend, but you'll get caught, with undeniable proof of fraud. Vincent Gramoli explains accountable consensus.

> You know, the consensus is central to blockchain, right? So making sure that, you know, there's a unique block at the given index prevents you from having double-spending. What we did back in 2019 was to define an accountable consensus version, which means that, okay, if you cannot solve consensus, which means that you probably have hackers among the participants that are trying to double-spend, and that's how you couldn't solve consensus, then what you can do if you truly solve the accountable consensus problem is that you can identify who they are. And so suddenly you make everyone accountable in the sense that, okay, you can cheat if you want, you can try to double-spend, but you're going to get caught. And there is no way around. There's going to be an undeniable proof of fraud that will be generated and everyone will see that you're responsible. If you know that, you're going to think twice before cheating because there is a risk that you cannot even double-spend, but for sure you're going to get caught.
