## 2. A tokenised invoice is just a PDF. The workflow is the value.

- **File:** [`clips/02-tokenised-workflows.mp4`](clips/02-tokenised-workflows.mp4) (subtitles also as [`subtitles/02-tokenised-workflows.srt`](subtitles/02-tokenised-workflows.srt))

- **Timestamps:** 05:40-06:10, 06:29-06:41
- **Speaker:** Vincent Gramoli, Founder, Redbelly Network
- **Topic:** Project Acacia / Redbelly technology (tokenised workflows)
- **Suggested post caption:** A tokenised invoice on its own is just a PDF in token form. Vincent Gramoli on why the value is in the workflow: a promise of payment you can use as collateral, as tested in Project Acacia.

> There is limited use in the tokenised assets, right? If you don't have the tokenised workflows around it, right? So if you think about, like, I remember some of the use cases we've done with Project Acacia and Arturo, which was about, you know, tokenising invoices, for example. So if you think about an invoice that is tokenised, okay, that's what, a PDF document that has been digitalised into a token? What's the value out of it? The value comes from the promise of a payment. An invoice is a promise of a payment, right? And that's really what is interesting here, because now you can use this invoice as a promise of payment as a collateral, maybe, you know, and you can borrow money. And this whole workflow is much more valuable than the digital representation of the invoice itself.

## 3. Try to double-spend and you get caught

- **File:** [`clips/03-accountable-consensus.mp4`](clips/03-accountable-consensus.mp4) (subtitles also as [`subtitles/03-accountable-consensus.srt`](subtitles/03-accountable-consensus.srt))
- **Source:** Redbelly Insights - Episode 28, 11 June 2026, [official episode file](https://storage.googleapis.com/redbelly-website.firebasestorage.app/Video/rb_ep_28_final_745719acdb/rb_ep_28_final_745719acdb.mp4)
- **Timestamps:** 11:49-11:56, 12:16-12:41, 12:42-12:58, 13:02-13:10
- **Speaker:** Vincent Gramoli, Founder, Redbelly Network
- **Topic:** Redbelly technology (accountable consensus)
- **Suggested post caption:** You can try to double-spend, but you'll get caught, with undeniable proof of fraud. Vincent Gramoli explains accountable consensus.

> The consensus is central to blockchain, right? So making sure that there's a unique block at the given index prevents you from having double-spending. What we did back in 2019 was to define an accountable consensus version, which means that, okay, if you cannot solve consensus, which means that you probably have hackers among the participants that are trying to double-spend, and that's how you couldn't solve consensus, then what you can do if you truly solve the accountable consensus problem is that you can identify who they are. And so suddenly you make everyone accountable in the sense that, okay, you can cheat if you want, you can try to double-spend, but you're gonna get caught. And there is no way around. There's gonna be an undeniable proof of fraud that will be generated and everyone will see that you're responsible. If you know that, you're going to think twice before cheating because there is a risk that you cannot even double-spend, but for sure you're going to get caught.

## 4. Every AI agent needs a human who answers for it

- **File:** [`clips/04-ai-agent-accountability.mp4`](clips/04-ai-agent-accountability.mp4) (subtitles also as [`subtitles/04-ai-agent-accountability.srt`](subtitles/04-ai-agent-accountability.srt))
- **Source:** Redbelly Insights - Episode 28, 11 June 2026, [official episode file](https://storage.googleapis.com/redbelly-website.firebasestorage.app/Video/rb_ep_28_final_745719acdb/rb_ep_28_final_745719acdb.mp4)
- **Timestamps:** 17:05-17:27, 17:35-17:56, 18:09-18:24
- **Speaker:** Vincent Gramoli, Founder, Redbelly Network
- **Topic:** Redbelly technology (application-layer accountability)
- **Suggested post caption:** AI agents are welcome, but someone has to answer for them. Vincent Gramoli on linking every Redbelly account to a real person or institution.

> There is yet another layer at the application level that consists of making sure that, for example, every account is linked to a human or an institution, as opposed to an AI agent. And I think it's very important. I'm not saying that Redbelly doesn't welcome AI agents. Of course, we're using that. But what I'm saying is that if there is an AI agent that is acting, he acts on the behalf of someone else, right? And by doing that, it should make sure that there is this person or institutions that acts, that should be responsible for its AI agent, right? So there is no full autonomy of the AI agent. And by having Redbelly Network associating an account to these kind of human or institutions that exist in the real world will guarantee that the AI agent who does something wrong will have a responsible party in the real world.
